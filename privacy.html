Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Revised: March 2026 • Bamboo Knit Reef

1. Introduction & Data Controller Overview

At Bamboo Knit Reef ("we," "us," "our," or the "Maison"), we recognize that your personal privacy is an indispensable cornerstone of our patron relationship. This Privacy Policy details our rigorous protocols for collecting, processing, storing, and safeguarding personal data obtained through your interactions with our website (bambooknitreef.com), our concierge communications, our bespoke consultations, and our direct allocation services in organic bamboo fiber hosiery and eco-knitwear.

For the purposes of the General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR), the UK Data Protection Act 2018, and the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), the designated Data Controller is:

2. Categories of Personal Information We Collect

Depending on how you engage with our Maison, we collect several categories of information to provide seamless deliveries, process custom orders, and enhance your digital experience:

  • Direct Identity & Contact Identifiers: Full name, prefix, shipping address, billing address, personal email address, telephone number, and bespoke allocation instructions provided during checkout or concierge inquiry.
  • Commercial & Transactional Data: Records of bespoke collections commissioned, specifications requested, invoice records, sizing or technical preferences, and communications with our customer concierge. (Note: We do not store raw payment card credentials; all payments are processed through PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateways).
  • Technical & Telemetry Data: Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser type and version, time zone setting, operating system, device identifiers, referring URLs, page interaction metrics, and clickstream data collected via secure cookies and Google Analytics (G-0LY0HY7L01).
  • Patron Preferences & Survey Data: Sizing preferences, custom inscriptions, feedback submitted via our quality assurance surveys, and delivery schedule preferences.

3. Lawful Bases for Processing (GDPR Compliance)

Under international data protection laws, we process your personal data strictly under valid lawful bases:

  • Contractual Necessity (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR): To fulfill our sales agreements, process transactions, execute bespoke commissions, coordinate insured carrier logistics, and manage warranty registries.
  • Legitimate Business Interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR): To monitor website performance, prevent fraudulent transactions, protect network security, conduct anonymized analytics, and improve our craft standards.
  • Legal Compliance (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR): To comply with financial accounting laws, tax reporting mandates, and consumer protection regulations.
  • Explicit Consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR): Where you have opted into receiving our seasonal monographs or analytical cookies. You may revoke consent at any time without penalty.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Processors

We hold patron confidentiality in the highest regard. We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information with third-party data brokers or marketing aggregators. Personal data is shared exclusively with vetted service providers operating under strict Data Processing Agreements (DPAs):

  • Logistics & Courier Partners: Trusted international carriers (e.g., DHL Express, FedEx, USPS) who receive shipping addresses strictly to execute physical deliveries.
  • Payment Infrastructure: Encrypted payment gateways adhering to PCI-DSS Level 1 security standards.
  • Analytics & Infrastructure: Google Analytics (configured with IP anonymization) and secure cloud server hosts located in compliant data centers.
  • Legal & Regulatory Authorities: When required by enforceable court orders, subpoenas, or applicable federal and state statutes.

5. International Data Transfers

Because our operations span our New York flagship atelier, European manufacturing facilities, and international logistics partners, your data may be transferred across international borders. When transferring data from the European Economic Area (EEA) or UK to the United States, we implement standard contractual clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission, ensuring equivalent levels of data protection across all jurisdictions.

6. Data Retention Protocols

We retain personal information only for the duration necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy:

  • Order & Transaction Records: Retained for seven (7) years to satisfy federal tax audit and statutory commercial recordkeeping mandates.
  • Concierge & Specification Records: Retained for the duration of your active patron relationship to facilitate effortless re-ordering and warranty servicing.
  • Anonymized Telemetry: Retained in aggregated statistical format for twenty-six (26) months via Google Analytics before automated purging.

7. Your Statutory Privacy Rights

Regardless of your geographic location, we respect your fundamental privacy rights. Under the GDPR, UK Data Protection Act, and CCPA/CPRA, you are entitled to:

  • Right of Access: Request a complete copy of all personal records we hold regarding your identity and transactions.
  • Right to Rectification: Request the correction of inaccurate or incomplete contact and delivery details.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"): Request the permanent deletion of your personal records, subject to statutory legal recordkeeping requirements.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request the temporary restriction of data processing while a dispute is being investigated.
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable JSON or CSV format.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Automated Profiling: We do not conduct automated profiling or algorithm-based decision making that produces legal effects.
  • California Non-Discrimination: California residents have the right to exercise privacy rights without experiencing price discrimination or altered service quality.

To exercise any of these statutory rights, please submit a formal request to our Data Protection Officer at privacy@bambooknitreef.com or call our concierge desk at +1-888-777-5845. We verify and respond to all verified requests within thirty (30) calendar days.

8. Information Security Architecture

We maintain comprehensive technical and organizational security measures to protect personal data against accidental loss, unauthorized access, destruction, or alteration. These measures include 256-bit TLS/SSL encryption across all web traffic, strict role-based employee access permissions, multi-factor authentication for administrative tools, and bi-annual third-party security audits.

9. Children's Privacy Notice

Our website and bespoke collections are intended exclusively for adults and young adults aged 16 and older. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 16. If we discover that a child under 16 has submitted personal records, we immediately purge the data from our active systems.

10. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect evolving regulatory frameworks or new collection features. Any material revisions will be prominently posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued engagement with our Maison following notice constitutes acknowledgment of the revised terms.
