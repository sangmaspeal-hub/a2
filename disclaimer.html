Bamboo Knit Reef

1. Sustainable Textile Claims

All bamboo fiber claims are backed by third-party OEKO-TEX Standard 100 and FSC organic forestry certifications. 1% of gross revenue is allocated directly to verified non-profit marine reef restoration programs.

2. Washing & Sizing

Bamboo knitwear conforms to natural foot contours after the first wash. Following our cool wash protocols guarantees longevity and maintains cloud-soft fiber luster.
