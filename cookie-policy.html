Digital Privacy & Storage

Cookie Preferences & Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Maison Bamboo Knit Reef

1. What Are Cookies and Tracking Technologies?

Cookies are small alphanumeric text files placed on your computer, smartphone, or mobile device when you visit a website. Cookies allow web applications to recognize your browser, retain shopping cart selections across navigation sessions, remember language preferences, and provide aggregated analytics regarding website performance.

In addition to standard cookies, modern web platforms may utilize local storage, session storage, and analytical tags (such as Google Analytics) to ensure smooth technical rendering of high-resolution imagery and interactive collection matrices.

2. Categories of Cookies We Utilize

At Bamboo Knit Reef, we believe in radical digital transparency. We categorize the cookies deployed on bambooknitreef.com into four distinct operational tiers:

Cookie Category Primary Operational Purpose Lifespan Opt-Out Status
Strictly Necessary Cookies Core site navigation, cart persistence, security tokens, SSL handshake Session to 12 Months Mandatory (Essential for site function)
Performance & Analytics Cookies Google Analytics (G-0LY0HY7L01) tracking page views, dwell time, load speeds 24 Months Configurable (Consent-based)

3. Google Analytics (G-0LY0HY7L01) & Telemetry Details

We utilize Google Analytics with strict privacy configurations to evaluate how patrons discover our collections and navigate our journal monographs:

  • IP Anonymization: We have enabled IP masking, which truncates the final octet of IPv4 addresses and the last 80 bits of IPv6 addresses before storage, ensuring your precise physical location cannot be identified.
  • Data Sharing Disabled: We have disabled Google data-sharing settings, preventing Google from utilizing our site analytics to benchmark external advertising networks.
  • No Cross-Device Tracking: We do not track user identities across multiple personal hardware devices.

4. Managing and Disabling Cookies in Your Browser

You have full control over cookie permissions on your device. Most modern web browsers allow you to manage cookie settings, refuse all cookies, or receive alerts when a cookie is being transmitted:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Block all cookies / Prevent cross-site tracking.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Enhanced Tracking Protection.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies.

Please note that if you completely disable strictly necessary cookies, certain essential features of our website (such as interactive tab switchers and secure consultation forms) may not function as intended.

5. Do Not Track (DNT) and Global Privacy Control (GPC) Signals

We honor modern privacy protocols. When our web servers detect a valid Global Privacy Control (GPC) or Do Not Track (DNT) browser header signal, we automatically disable all non-essential analytics and performance tracking cookies for that browsing session.

6. Updates to Our Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically to reflect technological enhancements or evolving global privacy regulations. Any updates will be published directly on this page with an amended effective date.

8. Technical Cookie Taxonomy & Functional Breakdown

To assist patrons in understanding our digital infrastructure, we maintain a complete technical inventory of browser storage keys utilized across our domain:

  • __cf_bm: Cloudflare security bot management token used to distinguish human patrons from automated scraping crawlers (Duration: 30 minutes).
  • _ga: Primary Google Analytics identifier used to distinguish unique visitors without storing personal identity records (Duration: 2 years).
  • _ga_0LY0HY7L01: Session state tracker retaining container property state across sequential page navigation (Duration: 2 years).
  • theme_preference: Local client storage key preserving contrast mode and high-resolution layout preferences across browsing sessions (Duration: Persistent).
  • session_cart_state: Secure cryptographic session token preserving active bespoke consultation forms and collection selections (Duration: Session-only).

9. Opt-Out Frameworks & Global Privacy Standards

Patrons in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, Switzerland, and California may dynamically adjust their tracking consent preferences at any time by clearing their local browser cache or submitting a formal opt-out request. We strictly adhere to the Interactive Advertising Bureau (IAB) Transparency & Consent Framework (TCF v2.2) and the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) Tracking Protection Working Group standards.
